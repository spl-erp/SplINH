WHATSAPP COEXISTENCE: OFFICIAL META DOCUMENTATION LINKS
Prepared 30 October 2026

Coexistence means one phone number is used on the WhatsApp Business app and on the Cloud API at the same time.


MAIN PAGE (read in full)

Onboard WhatsApp Business app users (Meta, official)
https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/embedded-signup/custom-flows/onboarding-business-app-users

chat history and contact sync, the webhook events (smb_message_echoes, history,
smb_app_state_sync, account_update), what is not supported, pricing note, 24-hour window rules,
and how to disconnect.

NOT OFFICIAL (provider documentation, not Meta's own words)

These facts appear only in provider documentation and were not found on any Meta page read:
- One phone number per coexistence account, and a maximum of four coexistence accounts per business.
- A waiting period of 1 to 2 months before reconnecting a number (Meta's page states no waiting period).
Source for the first point: Infobip
https://www.infobip.com/docs/whatsapp/manage-integration/coexistence


WHAT TO CONFIRM WITH META

- Meta's page says a Solution Partner or Tech Provider runs the onboarding. A route for a company
  connecting its own number is not described. This is the main thing to ask Meta or your contact.
- Whether a coexistence number counts toward the registered-number limit (2, rising to 20).
- Whether a coexistence number can send marketing through Meta's Marketing Messages API.
